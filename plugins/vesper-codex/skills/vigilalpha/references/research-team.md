# Bounded investment research in Codex

## Responsibilities and cost

The coordinator calls account tools once, owns the data cutoff and risk veto, and merges the result.
It can assign independent public-information tasks to a macro/event researcher and a company/
strategy researcher when delegation is available and authorized. A reviewer can inspect their short
evidence packets for contradictions; do not launch a reviewer for a simple factual lookup.
Use at most two concurrent research workers by default. This is a workflow instruction, not an
installed custom-agent runtime or a promise that every Codex host supports delegation.

Give workers a specific question, relevant public symbols, horizon, cutoff and output fields.
Keep account balances, position quantities, identifiers and credentials out of research prompts
and external search queries. No worker should repeat setup or refresh the account. A source shared
by multiple reports is one source, not independent corroboration. Cache or reuse dated evidence
when still fresh, and fetch only changes. Return a short evidence table, not copied articles.
Do not promise token savings: compare observed source/tool counts, latency and usage when available;
mark unavailable measurements honestly. If a worker fails, continue serially or show that gap.

## Evidence packet

Each material claim needs original URL, publisher, publication time, retrieval time, relevant market/
industry, fact/expectation/opinion label, limitation and conflicting evidence. Separate:

- Official facts: Fed decisions/calendar, BLS releases, SEC filings and company IR. A scheduled
  meeting is a known date; its future decision is unknown. A filing is an issuer statement.
- Market expectations: timestamped rates, yield curves, futures-implied expectations and analyst
  estimates from a verified source. Implied probabilities are model-dependent market expectations,
  never a central-bank promise or this plugin's calibrated forecast.
- Reported events/opinions: media coverage and analyst charts. Trace syndicated stories to their
  original source; do not average article sentiment into a buy probability.

Classify US coverage initially by equities/sector, rates/credit, FX/commodities and policy/events.
Report which categories and sectors are missing. Two media outlets cannot represent the entire US
market. Do not claim provider access, freshness, commercial rights or an installed integration from
its existence alone. Use connected tools or public official sources first; optional paid providers
retain separate authorization and credentials. A Trading 212 key cannot unlock another provider.

## Conditional decision card

Show: data cutoff; company thesis; valuation evidence or gap; month/week/day context when actually
available; relevant event and transmission channel; competing scenarios; portfolio constraints;
trigger, invalidation and next review condition. Different periods are correlated views, not votes.
Keep the runtime's research state and experimental maturity visible. Never invent prices, forecasts,
confidence percentages or fundamental scores to complete a card. Each holding gets evidence or a
specific gap; rendering a card is not complete analytical coverage.

Price/news scenarios are research proposals and cannot override runtime gates or generate an order.
For geopolitical uncertainty use observable triggers and revenue/cost/funding/FX channels. Historical
analogies are evidence to test, not forecasts. Quantities must come from the deterministic tool,
with the account data gate, currency and costs intact.

## Charts and personalization

Use the plugin's verified inline UI and text fallback. Keep chart suggestions honest: an exposure
diagram is not cash flow, an account balance is not a cash-flow-adjusted return curve, and projected
paths need explicit scenario labels. Unsupported sector/look-through data stays unknown. Do not
switch to the legacy dashboard or invent a new UI capability just to satisfy a chart request.
Horizon and risk preferences belong in the supported validated configuration; changing them never
promotes a strategy or overrides a risk veto.

## Learning and release

Obsidian is optional. Use the customer's selected Vault and its instructions; do not hard-code a
developer's private path into distributed behavior. Store sources, counterevidence, numerical
candidate rules and validation summaries, not account records. Scheduled research requires a real
host automation; installing this skill does not schedule anything.
Preserve the sequence: source -> candidate -> historical validation -> prospective observation ->
explicit human approval -> versioned release. Notes never become runtime parameters directly.
Record accepted and rejected hypotheses. Retain the existing promotion requirements, distinguish
operational sample counts from statistical adequacy, and never equate passing a gate with profit.
